## License Contradiction Resolution

The current MIT-only sale contract contradicted the intended paid single-use source-code model because MIT permits broad copying, modification, distribution, sublicensing, and resale. A sale package that claims single-use or no resale while relying only on MIT would be commercially incoherent.

The Owner's Option A resolves that contradiction fully for future paid CM01 sale packages:

- Paid buyers receive commercial use/modify/deploy rights from the WSTERA Commercial License / EULA.
- Paid packages can restrict redistribution or resale of the source as a standalone or competing module/template/source package.
- Historical MIT rights already granted are preserved and not retroactively revoked.
- Future WSTERA-owned releases can be packaged under the new commercial terms.

Result: **the sole locked Business / Market blocker is resolved at the decision level.**
