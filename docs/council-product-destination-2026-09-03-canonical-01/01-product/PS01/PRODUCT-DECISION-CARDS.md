# PS01 Pawstia — Product Owner Decision Cards

Purpose: minimal decision pack for the Product Gate remediation path. These are product-owner decisions, not implementation tasks.

## Card 1 — Public V1 Identity

Decision required: Confirm Pawstia V1 as **single-location Pet Hotel/Daycare OS**.

Default recommendation from synthesis: Use this identity for beta and owner-facing materials. Do not pitch V1 as a broad PMS, clinic/grooming/billing suite, multi-branch system, or marketplace.

Why it matters: All 3 candidates agree this is the sharpest evidence-backed product boundary.

## Card 2 — V1 Feature Boundary

Decision required: Lock the V1 promise around room/booking integrity, Daily Report to LINE, LINE claim, and Google Sheets ownership replica.

Default recommendation from synthesis: Treat dashboard, LIFF self-booking, camera, CSV import, entitlement/quota as support/enabling scope. Do not let them become the headline promise unless beta evidence says otherwise.

Why it matters: This prevents breadth creep before the real-store loop is validated.

## Card 3 — Visitor Camera

Decision required: Keep visible, hide, or postpone visitor camera for V1 beta.

Default recommendation from synthesis: Postpone or keep behind an internal/controlled flag unless a beta store explicitly values it.

Why it matters: Candidate evidence says camera is built/bounded, but not product-defining.

## Card 4 — First Closed-Beta Store

Decision required: Name store #1 and approve success metrics.

Minimum metrics: onboarding time, staff learning curve, real booking errors/conflicts, Daily Report creation time, LINE delivery success/failure, Sheets sync failure, support burden, owner perceived value, willingness-to-pay signal.

Why it matters: The Product Gate cannot pass on CI alone; all 3 candidates call out missing real-store evidence.

## Card 5 — Founding Member Offer

Decision required: Keep, revise, or hold the 990 THB Pro-forever Founding Member offer before WTP validation.

Default recommendation from synthesis: Hold public commitment until beta interviews and WTP evidence exist, unless owner intentionally accepts capped-upside risk.

Why it matters: Candidates flag this as a possible packaging trap before market evidence.

## Card 6 — Paid Launch Separation

Decision required: Confirm paid launch is blocked until separate commercial/ops/legal/brand gates close.

Default recommendation from synthesis: Keep payment and production launch out of Product Gate remediation. Product Gate should only prove identity, V1 boundary, and real-store value loop.

Why it matters: Avoids treating Phase 13 technical work as market fit or revenue readiness.

## Card 7 — LINE OA / Channel Ownership

Decision required: Choose beta and production posture for LINE OA/channel ownership.

Open options to decide: store-owned OA, Pawstia-managed OA, hybrid operator-assisted setup.

Why it matters: Daily Report delivery is product-defining; unclear channel ownership becomes onboarding and operations risk.
