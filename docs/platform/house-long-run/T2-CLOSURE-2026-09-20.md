## The digest contract (Codex-authorized remedy B)

The first attempt produced an unsatisfiable contract: `bodyDigest` was a **field of the body** while
also being compared to `sha256(rawBody)`. See
`CHAIN-FAILURE-T2-DIGEST-CONTRACT-2026-09-20.md`.

Codex classification (`T2-CLASSIFICATION-REPORT-CODEX-2026-09-20.md`): fingerprint correct;
contradiction real and unsatisfiable; **remedy B authorized as a bounded technical decision** —
remove the digest from the body, compute `sha256(rawBody)` server-side, and include that computed
digest in the canonical HMAC material.

Implemented exactly: the body schema deliberately has no `bodyDigest` field, the digest is an
explicit argument to `canonicalSigningPayload`, and a body that still carries `bodyDigest` is
rejected by the strict schema. The `X-Hub-Signature` transport header is unchanged.
