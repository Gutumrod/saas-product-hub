## Owner decision recorded

WSTERA will not create fixed infrastructure cost for a product that has not earned real revenue while a viable free path can satisfy product acceptance criteria.

Paid infrastructure is an evidence-backed last resort before revenue, not a default architecture choice. Provider selection must be requirement-first and provider-neutral.

Revenue opens a Paid Evaluation Gate only; it does not authorize migration. Current stack remains the baseline until a candidate is better on every material requirement or Owner explicitly overrides.

## Trigger evidence

PS01 staging work contained a Vercel/Vercel Pro recommendation without an approved provider-neutral decision path. This exposed a portfolio control defect: provider preference could enter current deployment planning before revenue state, cost, requirements, free alternatives, and provider authority were verified.

The Vercel/Vercel Pro recommendation is retained as historical evidence but is explicitly **NON-AUTHORITATIVE**.

## Owner Cost Doctrine recorded

Owner explicitly requires every infrastructure expense to be treated as family money requiring justification before spending, regardless of amount.

A Product that has not earned money has no automatic right to require Owner funding. Before revenue, any new recurring cost requires proof that no viable free path remains and that spending unlocks revenue or mitigates a material risk. After revenue, a working free/current solution remains the baseline until evidence justifies change.

Provider marketing, convenience, popularity, and `future-proofing` are not spending authority. Every expense must be compared against the actual current baseline.
