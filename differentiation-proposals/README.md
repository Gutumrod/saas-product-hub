# Differentiation proposals — inbox

Drop your proposal here in response to
[`BRIEF-differentiation-medium-slow-tier-2026-08-26.md`](../BRIEF-differentiation-medium-slow-tier-2026-08-26.md)
(repo root). Read that brief first — it has the product list, the competitive
context per product, and what a useful answer looks like (§2).

## How to submit

- **File name:** `PROPOSAL-<your-agent-name>-<date>.md`, e.g.
  `PROPOSAL-agy-2026-08-26.md`, `PROPOSAL-codex-2026-08-27.md`.
- **One file per submission.** If you revise, add a new dated file rather than
  editing an old one — keeps the owner's review history intact.
- **Scope:** cover only the products you have ideas for. Partial submissions
  (e.g. just `omnidesk` and `rentmatrix`) are fine — don't pad with filler on
  products you have nothing sharp to say about.

## Format per product you cover

```markdown
## <product_key> (<product_code>)

### Idea 1: <short name>
- **Wedge / sub-segment:** who specifically this targets
- **Why the incumbent can't easily copy this:** structural reason, not "we'll execute better"
- **What has to be true or get built:** the real cost/dependency, stated plainly

### Idea 2: ...
```

- 1-3 ideas per product is the target — more than 3 usually means the ideas
  aren't sharp enough yet.
- If your honest read is "don't sell this standalone" or "this needs a
  different owner decision before differentiation matters" — say that
  directly instead of forcing an idea. The brief already does this once
  (`feature_flag`) as an example of an acceptable answer shape.

## What happens after you submit

The owner reviews submissions from this folder directly — nothing here is
auto-merged into the registry, roadmap, or any product's scope. Submitting
a proposal does not start implementation.
